On-Campus Tutoring

The Tutoring Center is open for in-person services on campus, in Rooms 104/105 of the Cascade Learning Resource Center. Drop-In Tutoring is available in a variety of subjects--no appointment necessary!

eTutoring

eTutoring is available to all Everett Community College students. With eTutoring you will be connected virtually to a tutor supplied by a consortium of colleges throughout the United States. Most tutors you connect with will not be part of Tutoring Center staff or work for EvCC, but they will know the content and be able to help you!

You can find out which subjects are tutored at what days and times by viewing the weekly eTutoring schedule .
